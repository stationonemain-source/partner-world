Before you ask for an account, the honest part

  • Commission-only. There's no salary. Calls on their own pay nothing, and many businesses won't buy. What you earn depends on your effort, and results vary.
  • You earn from your own clients only. There's no recruiting, no team commissions and nothing to buy.
  • You're an independent partner, not a Station employee, and you must be 18 or older.
  • Before you're paid, you sign the Station Partner Agreement and upload a tax form (W-9, or W-8BEN outside the U.S.).
Questions

Straight answers

Is this a scam, or one of those pyramid things?

No. You earn 40% of what your own clients pay Station for real products. There's no recruiting, no team commission and nothing to buy. Station Automations Group LLC runs station.solutions, and you can email a real person at main@station.solutions before you ask for an account.

Do I need sales experience?

No. Partner World gives you the businesses to call, what to say for each product, and answers to the usual questions. Experience helps, but it isn't required.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. There's no fee and no starter kit.

How and when am I paid?

Once the client's payment has cleared. You earn 40% of what each client pays Station: for a website, 40% of the setup fee (hosting and care earn nothing); for everything else, 40% of every payment while they keep paying and your partner agreement is in effect. Payouts go out once you have $50 or more; a smaller balance rolls forward to the next payout and never expires. Payment starts once your signed agreement and tax form (W-9, or W-8BEN outside the U.S.) are on file.

Where do the businesses come from?

Station gives them to you in Partner World. You don't have to find or buy leads.

How much time does it take?

As much as you choose. There's no quota and no schedule. Calls happen 9 am to 8 pm in the business's time zone, Monday to Saturday.

What happens after I ask for an account?

There's no application to fill in. Station looks at your request by hand; if it approves you, you get an email, and you sign in with the email and password you chose when you asked.

Ready to ask?

It takes about a minute. Nothing happens until Station approves you.
